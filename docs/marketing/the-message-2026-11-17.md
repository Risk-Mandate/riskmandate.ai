## 4. Notes

**Recommended: Draft A.** It is the lead's order in one breath (control, a policy first, hooks and SHarness, open
source, "your person"), and its one figure is our own team, the proof the lead asked to lead with.
Use C where the reader should click before talking (a post); B where a link cannot be carried (spoken, a bio).

**What each draft deliberately does not claim:**

- **All three.** No customer, no named person, no score, no conformity word. They do not say the ABP stops an
  attacker (the pricing page says plainly it does not), nor that hooks or SHarness bind every agent (the home page
  says "nothing yet binds the agent itself" of SHarness). "Control" is the lead's word; none of the drafts says
  *enforce*, *guarantee* or *secure*.
- **Draft A.** It says we *do it on our own team*, not that every summit agent was blocked or held: the summit ABPs
  were in observe mode, gaps logged and allowed apart from one call, and most were documented rather than measured.
  It also does not say "each with its own ABP": the stand deck of 10 Nov shows 109 agents, 94 with a written policy
  (https://web-summit.sgit.ai/meetings/2026-11-10-1600/show/). If A is used where there is room, add "in observe
  mode" after the link.
- **Draft B.** No figure and no proof link, so it claims nothing measurable; it should travel with a link to the home
  page or the situations page wherever one fits.
- **Draft C.** "We show our working" points at the free library only; it does not claim the sixteen templates were
  measured (most rows are documented from the vendor's pages, not run).

**For the lead (two small things found while drafting):**

1. The lead said "we are your person"; the home page closes with "we are your people". The drafts use the lead's
   word. Which one the site keeps is the lead's call.
2. The home page's opening says "more than a hundred agents ..., each with its own Agent Behaviour Policy (ABP)",
   while its own stand-deck figure says 94 of 109 had a written policy. A developer task, not a message one;
   flagged for the conductor.
