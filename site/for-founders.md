# Diligence will find it. You can find it first.

You will be diligenced on your agents before long, by an enterprise customer, an acquirer or an investor. The useful direction is not a posture document that says you are fine. It is the document that says what you can actually reach, written by you, before somebody else writes a worse version of it.

## Would you like to know what diligence finds, before it runs?

## The reassuring version is already free.

Buy-a-posture-document is a solved and crowded problem, and a document asserting that everything is under control is worth what any self-assessment is worth. We are not selling you that. A behaviour policy is useful precisely because it can be unflattering: the delta is computed, so it says what you can reach and did not ask for whether or not that is convenient.

## A row somebody can argue with beats an assurance.

Diligence questions about agents are new enough that most answers are improvised. Arriving with the enumeration already done, including the parts that are not tidy, changes the conversation from whether you have thought about it to which rows matter. The ones that are unflattering are evidence you did the work rather than evidence you have a problem.
