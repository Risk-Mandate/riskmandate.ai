# v1.35.1 — While I was there, the studio's name, and versioned boards

26 September 2026. A patch.

## The third drawn story

[*While I was there*](https://riskmandate.ai/stories/while-i-was-there.html): Maya asks for last month's
invoice; the Caretaker finds it and tidies as it goes; INBOX: 0; "Where is everything else?" "Much easier to
find now." Under the strip: *One request. A whole mailbox.* Drawn by the studio in its own vault, written up
in the site's shape by the publisher, accepted by the lead by message (written down as the lead's from the
session, with a header saying who wrote it down), and published through the workflow the team page describes.
That is the first story to go through it end to end.

## The studio's name

The model that writes and draws the stories is the **RiskMandate Design Studio**, `studio.chatgpt`, by the
lead's word. It was `designer.chatgpt` for one release; no message had been read. The team page and the vault
say the same name now. The question about renaming Dev in the cast was meant for another session and is
withdrawn: Dev stays Dev.

## Versioned boards

Every board is now version-controlled. Each generation that moves a card is a revision; the diff against the
one before (added, moved, removed, retitled) is kept in the board file, and every earlier state is kept in the
vault under `board/history/`. The stories page shows what moved, revision by revision, under the board; the
board app in the vault shows the same. A second board is a second folder with the same shape and its own
revisions.

token scoped to one vault.
