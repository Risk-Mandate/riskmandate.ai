## The business case for Cloudgeni and Opengeni

After a call with one of Cloudgeni's founders, a pilot: the agents behind RiskMandate's Gmail and
Calendar behaviour policies, run inside Opengeni, the open-source harness Cloudgeni runs on. The
lead's note is [D26](briefs.html). The case was written before the pilot, from the public pages
only, read on 25 September 2026.

- **[The case](business-case-cloudgeni.html)**, computed like the others. Six things the two
  products do in their own words. Five answers changed for an infrastructure agent: changes need a
  person, a boundary because the deployment credential is not in the agent's grant; what it did is
  replayable; it can be stopped in one action, in minutes; and its reach is stated at dispatch, for
  a run in a managed sandbox. Each says which holder and which kind. The register before and after.
  What it adds, including an Azure role the setup page assigns and does not explain. What adopting
  it takes. Two places the pages disagree: whether a routine change ever merges on its own, and
  which clouds. What the case does not claim, including anything from the conversation.
- **The harness against the Gmail ABP, row by row.** Our published Gmail policy has four rows of
  unbounded excess. For each, what Opengeni's pages would let a run inside it do: the send becomes
  an approval the operator holds, the host rows leave a sandboxed run's grant, the schedule row
  depends on which surface the pilot connects the mailbox through. The projection: unbounded
  excess from four to nought, with two residual risks recorded where there were none. The Calendar
  before-image, kept in the harness's replayable log, is named as the pilot's first design decision.
- **From the buyer's desk**: one run from the task to the pull request, as a figure, and five
  points the person who signs would want.
- **Six notes on the pilot and the case as a product**: the measured Gmail ABP as the first
  deliverable; the Calendar before-image as the demonstration; selling the harness by the answers
  it changes; a vault per run as the evidence pack; open source the way open-source.sgit.ai
  describes it; and what not to claim.
- A new category, agent harnesses and runtimes. A case may now name its own heading for the
  market section.

What was not done: the measurement, which is the pilot's; the Calendar policies, still in draft;
and the message to Cloudgeni, which is the lead's.
