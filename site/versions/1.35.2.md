# v1.35.2 — A behaviour policy for a ChatGPT dot

30 September 2026. A patch: one article, and one entry on the asked-for list.

## The article

[*Your dot has its own computer, its own browser and your apps. Write down what it was for.*](https://riskmandate.ai/article-abp-for-chatgpt-dots.html)
OpenAI released dots on 29 September 2026: always-on agents inside ChatGPT with a cloud computer, a browser, and
every app the account ever connected, working between conversations. The article introduces them for readers who
have not met one, from the vendor's own pages, and then does what the site does: twelve capability rows in the
vocabulary, each with the vendor's sentence, a barrier and who holds it; the finding that the strongest barriers on
the rows that matter, sending, changing, deleting, spending, are held by a reviewer model the vendor itself calls a
judgement and not a guarantee, recorded as a boundary with the caveat beside it and filed as a request to the model
site for a word for that kind; six questions the pages leave open; a starting mandate with its delta derived
(grant 12, mandate 5, excess 7, unbounded 2); and five steps to write one for your own dot.

Nothing was tested: dots are not offered to Pro accounts in the UK, and a row becomes measured only on a system we
are entitled to run. Eight of the vendor's pages were read and quoted on 30 September; two refused the site's
tooling and were read through a page-reader service against the same addresses, which the article says.

## The asked-for list

`chatgpt-dots` joins the shapes people have asked a behaviour policy for, marked as documented in the article and
not yet a vault. The twelve rows and the six questions are ready for one.
