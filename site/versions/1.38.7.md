# v1.38.7 — The review page fits a phone

9 October 2026. The lead's first review from a phone, sent through the page with a screenshot pasted into
the comment, showed the list of changes squeezed to one word per line and the page wider than the screen.

- **The cause:** below 900 pixels the list of changes drops to two columns, a number and the text, but the new
  text fell into the number's 26-pixel column. It now sits under the old text, at full width, and long words
  wrap instead of widening the page. Checked with no element wider than the screen at 375, 430 and 820
  pixels.
- **The feedback that found it** arrived as it should: four messages, each signed by the phone's own key, with
  the screenshot sent as a JPEG (Safari does not write WebP, so the page fell back) and filed in the review
  vault, whose dashboard was rebuilt and pushed.

Checked in Chromium at phone sizes, not on the phone itself.
