# v1.36.0 — The new home page

2 October 2026. The lead: *the new version is signed off.* And a question, answered below: with these latest changes,
do we still need the backup of the current home page?

- **The experiment is the home page.** What was `home-next.html` from v1.35.14 to v1.35.17 is now `index.html`: the
  design the lead sent as a React component, ported to plain HTML, CSS and one short script, in the site's chrome.
  The hero; the stage with six agents, the gate and the four kinds of dot; the eight systems; the builder; bring your
  policy; a prompt asks, a policy enforces; the phone and the email; the four levels, the store's, in pounds, with
  level 3 selected and the free early-access link; the model, the previous home page's own blocks, the gap figure
  with its counts, the four parts, the four views and the flow, the three steps; and the closing call. The note that
  called it an experiment is gone, the page is indexed again, and it has its markdown twin.
- **One block came back from the old page for the swap:** the proof strip under the stage, four claims with their
  links into the store's ledger. It carries the count of published templates, and the tests hold that count, the four
  numbers on the gap figure and the link to the behaviour policy they come from, true against the catalogue and the
  vault on every build. The pitch-deck renderer reads the gap figure from this page, and still finds it.
- **No, the backup is not needed, and it is gone.** `home-current.html` was a copy of the old home page so the two
  could be opened side by side. The comparison is over, and git holds the old page at every version it ever had;
  v1.35.17 is the last with it at the root. `home-next.html` stays as a one-line redirect to the root, because the
  link was shared, and both entries leave the menu. The second number moves, 1.35 to 1.36, because the home page is
  what the site is.
- **Still the lead's call, unchanged from the v1.35.14 review:** the design's enforcement language, real time, alerts
  by text and email, a tamper-evident decision log, describes a product the site does not sell today. It is on the
  home page now, as signed off, and the list of those lines is in the v1.35.14 notes for when the founders decide.

Not done: the design's font, Geist, is not loaded; the page sets in the system sans like the rest of the site.
