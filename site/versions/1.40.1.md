# v1.40.1 — The ABP's open home: the Open Security Summit, in place of OWASP

3 November 2026. On 20 October RiskMandate's founders proposed that the Agent Behaviour Policies become the first
hosted project of the Open Security Summit, a UK community interest company, instead of an OWASP project. The lead
asked for the OWASP section to come off the site's top level and for the process of replacing it to start.

- **OWASP leaves the menu.** Its eight pages stay at their addresses, unlisted, as the record of the 8 October
  proposal: the newsroom and the brief register link them. Each now opens with a banner saying it was superseded
  on 20 October and where the proposal went, drawn from one field in the record.
- **Nothing was sent to OWASP.** The committee email, the project application and the note to the Agent Control
  Standard maintainers were all drafts, and stay drafts. The record's stage, decisions (K05) and log say so; nothing
  earlier in it was rewritten.
- **A new page, `open-security-summit.html`, under Behaviour policies:** why the ABP needs a home that is not
  RiskMandate's; the proposal, a host, a programme of hosted projects and the ABP as the first; the governance that
  has to come first, a public charter and a steering group with a majority independent of RiskMandate and The Cyber
  Boardroom; what is stewarded and what is not; RiskMandate's place as a founding contributor and one implementer
  among others; the four phases, each marked proposed; and what carries over from the OWASP work, the 82-asset
  inventory and the draft charter.
- **A brief for the process** (`direction__the-abp-moves-to-the-open-security-summit.md`): what the site did, the
  phases with who does each and the site's part when it happens, and what the site must not say until it is true.
- **The LinkedIn desk** gains a post for the move, sixteen items in all.

What stays: OWASP's own projects on this site (the OWASP graph, the Coraza and Threat Dragon business cases), the
OWASP Agentic Top 10 among the standards, and the lead's OWASP history; they are about OWASP, not about where the ABP
lives. What was not done: none of the four phases has started as far as this site records, and the page says
*proposed* on each; the ABP is not described as an Open Security Summit project or as independently governed.
