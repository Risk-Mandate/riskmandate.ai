# v1.41.23 — Questions: what a regulated buyer can use, and what we do not do

18 November 2026.

- **[Questions](questions.html) gains Q04**, for a buyer in health or finance: a behaviour policy needs none of the
  regulated data, is read-only and never sits in the agent's path, and a draft needs no access to anything of theirs; it is
  not a compliance assessment and not legal advice. For the vaults themselves, it gives the limits the lead stated in
  [Patient vaults, answered](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/patient-vaults-answered.html) (17 November), and says those
  questions were asked of the sgit project, not of us.

What was not done. No new claim; the answer says only what the article and this site already say.
