# v1.35.9 — The ultimate insider, drawn

2 October 2026. A patch: one story moves from storyboard to drawn.

[*The ultimate insider*](https://riskmandate.ai/stories/the-ultimate-insider.html) was drawn by a ChatGPT image model
from the prompt on its page, one day after the storyboard went up, and the lead relayed the result: two sheets, panels
1 to 10 and panels 11 to 14. They are on the page with six notes on what to correct, written beside them rather than
painted over: the sheets instead of single panels; the Caretaker's face, which drifted from the cast sheet; a plaque in
panels 12 and 13 reading WITHIN POLICY with a tick, which breaks two of this site's rules at once and should be redrawn;
a register title and sentence the model invented; one blue sticker where two were asked for; a narration line the model
added; and the re-set wordmark. Everything else holds: the cast, the keys, the clocks, the three windows on Leo's
console, the licence on the wall, the dashboard, the car with its brake lights.

The builder now lets a long story be drawn as several sheets. The delivery and the acceptance are in the vault as
messages, and the review has gone to the studio.
