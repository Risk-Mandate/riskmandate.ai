# v1.35.11 — The offer, not the gift

2 October 2026. A patch of one word, at the lead's reading.

The early-adopters page called the corrected behaviour policy a gift. It is an offer: that is what it is, that is the
word that pairs with a thirty-day window and a form, and it is the word the emails in the console brief now use too.
The hero's other line, *what we owe you*, stays, because that is why.
