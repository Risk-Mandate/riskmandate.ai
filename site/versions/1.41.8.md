# v1.41.8 — The menu works on the summit and archive pages; the retired prices are gone from every product page

17 November 2026. Four synthetic users (a CISO, a founder with ten agents, an underwriter, a developer) went through the
live site this morning; their report is in `docs/marketing/synthetic-users-2026-11-17.md`. This release fixes the four
findings that were ours to fix today.

- **The menu works on every page in a folder.** On the Web Summit 2026 pages and the archive the menu built its links
  from the folder, so all forty went to a 404. Those pages now tell the menu where the root is, and a new test fails the
  build if a page in a folder does not.
- **The retired prices are gone from the product pages.** [For corporates](for-corporate.html),
  [for founders](for-founders.html), [for start-ups](for-startups.html) and [Licence to Operate](licence-to-operate.html)
  still offered the £5 pack and the £50 vault; each now shows the four ways in, £500 to £20,000. [What an ABP is](abp.html)
  sends its button to the four ways in rather than to the retired pack.
- **Pricing names its ways in one way.** Below the table the page still said *level 3* and *level 4*; it now says the £500
  policy and the £1,500 way in, matching the table.
- **The header button says what it does.** On the home page and the new pages it read *Get in touch* but opened the
  tutorial site; it now reads *Try it free*, as on every other page.

What was not done. The store still lists the £10 and £50 levels and says the payment links for the others are not issued;
the store belongs to another team and the lead is told. A page a developer can start from, the insurance pages linking the
real data, and the team page's count of agents are the loop's next items.
