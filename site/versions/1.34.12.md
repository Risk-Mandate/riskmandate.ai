## How to read the visualiser

The lead asked for a thorough article on the risk propagation visualiser alone ([D24](briefs.html)):
lots of captures, strategic nodes clicked and paths highlighted, in the order a business reads it.

- **[One agent, and every desk it reaches](article-risk-propagation-visualiser.html)**, in that
  order. Seven user stories, one per desk the picture is for. The six weeks as a story, with the
  captures of each day. How to read the six bands, the two colours and a click. Each connection
  on its own, CRM, Mail and Calendar, with what each grants and who holds what; then combined,
  where R2 appears, a risk none of them has alone. Eight controls one at a time and then all of
  them, with a table of what each does to the record, what it ends, what it leaves in green and
  where unbounded excess goes. The business analysis, and five questions to ask with it on a
  screen. The technical analysis: the data, the three functions that decide what holds, the
  acceptance record, the chain and the colour, the one-way walk, the drawing, and what it would
  take to run it on a real agent.
- **Thirty-four captures**, each taken by a script that set the figure to the state the caption
  names and photographed it. They are WebP, about 1.6 MB together, in
  `site/assets/articles/risk-propagation/`.
- The figure's own page now links to the guide.

What was not done: captures of a real agent, because the figure runs on the invented example; and a
capture for every one of the map's roles.
