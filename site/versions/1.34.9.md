## Two circles on a role

One change to the blast radius, from the lead: a role with ten risks reaching it and three of
them red read as ten red.

- **A role now carries up to two circles.** A red one counts the unaccepted risks that reach it,
  held or carried; a black one counts the accepted. One circle when only one kind arrives, two
  side by side when both do. On day 4 of the six weeks the board reads *1 · 8*: one risk moving
  up the chain unaccepted, eight accepted below it. With every control on it reads *9*, all black.
- That is the question the figure is for: how many risks arrive at each person, at each altitude,
  and how many of them nobody has yet signed for.
