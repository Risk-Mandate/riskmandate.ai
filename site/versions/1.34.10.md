## Click, and the rest fades

Four small changes to the blast radius, from the lead, so that it reads the way a person uses it:
click a thing and see what it touches.

- **A click sticks, and the rest fades.** Click a role, a risk, a connection or the assistant and
  everything it touches keeps its colour: the facts and connections below it, and every role on
  the path up to the board. Everything else fades, in the picture and in the list of risks under
  it. Click it again, or the background, to see everything. A hover only previews the panel.
- **The connections and the assistant have a panel too.** Click Mail, CRM, Calendar or the
  assistant and the panel says what it grants and why it cannot be narrower (the consent lines
  cover the whole account; the CRM login is the rep's), which rows are in the grant now, which
  risks hold because of it, and which roles they reach.
- **The selection is visible.** A selected role, risk, connection or the assistant carries a
  dark outline. Before, only a hovered role changed at all.
- **Nothing blinks.** The ring on an unaccepted risk used to pulse for ever; it is a still ring
  now. The travelling dots remain, and only when the record changes.
