# v1.41.20 — One story on every page: every way in starts with a conversation, levels named by price

17 November 2026. Our sherpa agent walked the live site as a stranger with a few agents and one worry, and wrote down
where the path broke (`docs/marketing/sherpa-walk-2026-11-17.md`). This release fixes the seven breaks that were ours.

- **[Pricing](pricing.html)** no longer offers to sell anything at the store: the buttons are *Talk to us*, the first
  step of the £500 policy is writing to us, and the after-you-pay table names each way in by its price.
- **[The summit record](web-summit-2026/)** no longer says every agent had a policy: most did, 94 of the 109 on the
  circuit on 10 November, linked, as the home page says.
- **[How it works](how-it-works.html)** no longer says there is no enforcement point. The behaviour policy itself only
  reads and never sits in the agent's path, and we are never in the request path; enforcement, when you want it, is a
  hook in your own harness ([for developers](developers.html)) or a boundary such as [SHarness](secure-harness.html).
- **Levels are named by price**, not by number, on [the reviewed level](abp-reviewed.html), how it works and
  [early adopters](early-adopters.html).
- **The four audience pages** lose the sentence about downloading the retired pack, and their store buttons become
  *Talk to us*. [UK support](uk-support.html) says all four ways in start with a conversation.
- **Archived pages say so.** [Startup Summit](summit.html) and the after-payment pages for the two retired levels carry
  the dated archive note.

What was not done. The menu, the header button and the store are the lead's or another team's, and are asked about.
