# v1.41.19 — For developers: agents near money, the lead's position as a behaviour-policy line

17 November 2026.

- **[For developers](developers.html#money)** gains an example for agents that work near payments, from the lead's
  article of 17 November, [the payment desk](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/the-payment-desk.html): the money stays in
  plain, auditable code, and the agent works on the change around it; agents may get single-use, budgeted wallets once
  trusted. As a line: *never initiate, approve or change a payment; payments run in code a person reviews.*

What was not done. It is labelled as what it is: a position and an illustration, not a product, and the line has not
been tested.
