# v1.35.15 — The experiment prices what we can deliver: the four levels

2 October 2026. The lead, after showing the experimental home page to Nimay, who agreed: *one thing we need to change
on this page is those prices, since at the moment we are not set up to deliver and manage monthly and yearly policies.
For now we continue with our four offerings, which we can deliver now, and get the signals from our early users on
what they would buy, and at what price.*

- **The price builder on `home-next.html` is gone.** The mock priced a product per system, per month or per year, in
  dollars, with a running total; none of that is something the company can deliver or manage today. The section keeps
  the design, the two-column layout with the chips on the left and the dark card on the right, and fills it with the
  store's four levels: the pack for £10, the working vault for £50, corrected for your situation for £500, two
  sessions and a professional's signature for £1,500. Pick a level and the card says who it is for, what arrives, who
  does the work, and carries the store's own buy link for that level. Prices are in pounds, per agent, per deployment,
  paid once; no subscription.
- **The page says what comes next in the founders' words:** these four are what we can deliver today; what comes
  next, and at what price, we will learn from the people using these, and the early-access programme is where that
  conversation happens.
- **The note at the top no longer calls the prices the design's**, because they are now the store's. Its copy and
  product claims are still the design's and still under review.
- One dollar figure remains on the page, inside a rule in the system picker, *raise ad spend by more than $500 a
  day*; that is a rule about an agent's spend, not a price, and stays as the mock wrote it.

Not done: the rest of the mock's copy, enforcement, alerts by text and email, is as it was; the founders have the
list from v1.35.14 and have not yet said what stays.
