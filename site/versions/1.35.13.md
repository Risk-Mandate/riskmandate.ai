# v1.35.13 — The company as a side note, and CC BY on the deck

2 October 2026. Two corrections from the lead, by voice, an hour after v1.35.12 went live.

- **The Cyber Boardroom steps back to a side note.** v1.35.12 opened the about page with *The Cyber Boardroom is the
  company behind RiskMandate*, its mark beside ours. The lead's correction: The Cyber Boardroom is a company
  incorporated in the UK with a product of its own; RiskMandate's early trading runs through it today, and a company
  for RiskMandate alone, between the two founders, is being set up. So the about page now leads with the two founders
  and the document, and the company sits in one sourced line at the end of the first section, with a link. The line
  is said in full only on the early-adopters page, where it matters, because the people that page thanks were The
  Cyber Boardroom's early users. That page is unchanged.
- **Confidential becomes CC BY 4.0 on the deck.** The lead asked for the word to go. The deck is a Chromium print in
  which every glyph is an outlined path, so there was no font to set new text in; slide 15 carries *under CC BY 4.0.*
  at the footer's size, so the new footer is built from the deck's own letterforms: the eleven paths of *Confidential*
  dropped, the seven of *CC BY 4.0* copied in on the same baseline, the dot and page number shifted left to close the
  gap. Thirteen slides carried the word; the five whose footer names its sources did not, and are untouched. Nothing
  else on any slide changed, and the published file is now smaller than the one received, because the rewrite
  recompressed it. The script is `scripts/deck/relabel-investor-deck-footer.py`, with the deck as presented as its
  input, so the change is reproducible and a v4 can be relabelled the same way. The eighteen slide images on the
  about page are re-rendered from the new file.
- **The page's note on the footer** now says what happened rather than what was there: as presented the footer said
  *Confidential*; the lead chose to publish and asked for CC BY; nothing else was touched. The deck's page numbers,
  01 to 17 and then 19, are still the file as presented.

Not done: the early-adopters page still names The Cyber Boardroom as the company behind RiskMandate, on purpose; the
legal name of the new company is not known yet and nothing on the site guesses it.
