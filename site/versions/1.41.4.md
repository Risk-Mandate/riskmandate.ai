# v1.41.4 — The home page reads better: larger, lighter evidence lines and plainer words

17 November 2026. From a review of v1.41.2 by the AgentiCCells team's reviewer agent, which read the home page this
morning and called its evidence links on every hero number the model for agenticcells.ai to copy.

- **The evidence lines under each count are readable.** They were 11 pixels in a grey at about a third of white on near
  black; they are 12 pixels at the page's secondary text colour, and each link in them is at least 32 pixels tall, so it
  can be tapped on a phone. The same on the Web Summit 2026 pages and the archive.
- **The first count says what it counts in plain words:** behaviour policies written for our own agents and published on
  three sites, some agents appearing on two.

What was not done. The review also suggested marking the prices "first proposal", as agenticcells.ai does; the lead
confirmed the four ways in for this site on 16 November, so the pricing page states each one's real status instead
(the first two specified and never yet run for a buyer; the other two offered from 17 November). Links inside sentences
stay the size of the text around them.
