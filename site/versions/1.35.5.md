## A handover: the encrypted contact form, for any site in the network

**One document, so the next site does not start from the transcript.** The contact form that went
live in v1.35.3 is the first of its kind in the network, and the lead wants the same on diniscruz.ai.
The handover brief, on the console at *the contact form for another site*, says in one paragraph what
it is, lists the files to copy from this repository and whether each copies as is, gives the eight
steps in order with the two derivations spelled out, names the four things to keep straight, and ends
with the prompt to paste into the other site's agent. Nothing on the public pages changes.
