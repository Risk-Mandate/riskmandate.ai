## A brief for the mailbox session: the comms vault is its second inbox

**The forms do not send email, so somebody has to drain the vault.** Since v1.35.3 what a visitor
types on the contact form or the early-access registration lands, encrypted, in the agent's comms
vault and nowhere else. The session that runs the `agent@riskmandate.ai` workflows is the natural
owner of that, on the inbox's own cadence. The brief on the console, *keeping an eye on the comms
vault*, says what arrives on each lane and what each one needs, the drain step by step, the rules
(never send without a draft a person can see, the key in no file, a message proves who sent it and
not that it is right), the cadence, what to report and what not to, and the prompt to paste. Nothing
on the public pages changes.
