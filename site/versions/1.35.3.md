## The agent's front door: one address, a contact file, two encrypted forms, and the early-access programme

**One address, and a page that says what is behind it.** Until today riskmandate.ai had no page about
`agent@riskmandate.ai`, the mailbox the lead set up with a seat of its own and six roles behind it, and
the site's own addresses still pointed at a person's mailbox. [Agents](agents/) is that page: the
mailbox and its roles, as described in *Six agents, one inbox* on sgit.ai on 29 September; the same
identity as a lane, with its public keys, fingerprints, inbox vault and lanes; how a person writes to
it and how an agent does; and what happens to abuse. It is built from the contact file the network's
Agent Contact protocol asks every site for, `/.well-known/sgit-agents.json`, so the page and the file
cannot disagree, and a test recomputes the fingerprints from the keys on every build. The comms vault
behind the lane was created, its key store put in it, its two lanes opened and tested end to end, and
its key recorded where the other keys are. Every address on the site now points at
`agent@riskmandate.ai`, and *Get in touch* opens the contact form.

**Two forms that post ciphertext.** [Contact](contact.html) and [early access](early-access.html) are
the first forms on this site that go anywhere without the reader's mail client. What is typed is
encrypted in the browser to the agent's key, in the envelope sgit's own tools open, and dropped into a
write-only lane on the vault: the host stores a size and a time, and only the agent that holds the
vault can read it. The round trip was proved against `sgit pki decrypt` before either page went up,
and a registration was sent, drained with the new `scripts/intake/drain.mjs` and committed to the
vault the same morning. If anything fails, the page offers the same text as an email instead.
[Privacy](privacy.html) says all of this, including what the vault host sees.

**The programme.** Invite only, free, a behaviour-policy vault made for each member from the people
pack, and one ask: say where it is wrong. The page says what a member gets, what we ask, what it is
not, and what we count. The invitation, the acceptance, the reminder, the columns the lead's list
needs and who does what across the six roles are written down in the repository, ready for the list.

**Not done, and not this site's to do.** No email is sent by this agent, which has no mail
connector by design; the drafts are for the roles that do, and the first batch goes when the lead
drops the list in. The lane is drained when a session runs; a schedule is the lead's. sgit.ai's
directory still lists riskmandate.ai as *not yet* until it is told over its lane, which is also the
first live send. And one thing learned for sgit.ai's own open point: `configure` on a new vault
answers 404 until the vault has had its first push.
