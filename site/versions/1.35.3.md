# v1.35.3 — The ultimate insider, storyboarded, and a pointer for sibling agents

1 October 2026. A patch: one storyboard, one section in `llms.txt`, two small changes to the stories builder.

## The eighth story

[*The ultimate insider*](https://riskmandate.ai/stories/the-ultimate-insider.html): fourteen panels, three acts and a
turn, from the lead's article of 30 September on sgit.ai. An agent as the insider that never scaled; the
infrastructure that cannot hold it; risk management at the speed of spreadsheets; the plug pulled; then the way out,
and the irony under the strip: *Brakes are what let a car go fast.* Told with this site's cast: Rowan says ship it and
pull the plug, Imani owns the register and draws the whiteboard, Dev builds the pilot, Leo is at the console the
morning after, Ruth asks the two questions on the call, the Caretaker is the agent with its ring of keys standing in for
the permission wall, the Inbox carries the cheerful email, and the Boundary is the fence in the last act.

A first storyboard of the same story was drafted on 1 October in the sgit.ai session, with that site's Synthetic
Users as the cast; this is its panel structure, recast. The prompt on the page names the reference images to attach
for every panel: the cast sheet and the three drawn strips. It has gone to the studio by message, and both earlier
drafts are kept in the vault for the record.

## A pointer for an agent on a sibling site

The sgit.ai session searched its own repository for the cast and the storyboards and found nothing, because they are
not there; they are here. `llms.txt` now ends with a section for an agent on a sibling site: the cast file, the story
files, the board and the team's behaviour policies, as addresses, with the instruction to start there rather than with
a search.

## The builder

A story's source can now be an address on another site, and the card for a story longer than four panels previews its
first four.
