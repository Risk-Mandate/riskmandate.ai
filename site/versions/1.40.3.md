# v1.40.3 — Three entries and one button: the menu, after a partner's review

4 November 2026. One of RiskMandate's founders reviewed the home page on 3 November and asked for three navigation
links and one call to action, with the rest moved down rather than lost. The lead agreed with the direction: keep
everything we have, and push it down the menus.

- **The header is Product, How it works, Open source, and Try it free.** It used to have six dropdowns, two icons, the
  version chip and the button. Every page that was in the menu still is, one level down, in a titled column:
  - **Product:** the Agent Behaviour Policy and its levels, who it's for, and *the roadmap*. The roadmap holds Licence
    to Operate, the Insurability Index and the insurance pages. They are labelled as where this goes, not as what is
    sold today.
  - **How it works:** *see it work* (how it works, the sixteen examples, the grant against the mandate, try it, the
    live demos, the Lab) and *read* (articles, the newsroom, stories, the library, questions).
  - **Open source:** *the open project* (SHarness, the code on GitHub, the Open Security Summit, our own agents'
    behaviour policies, the OWASP projects graph, the brand) and *company and community* (about, partners, UK support,
    contact, feedback, privacy, the agents' contact file).
- **One place still draws it.** `pages.json` gains a `section` per page, and the shared menu module draws a dropdown's
  sections as columns, two at laptop width, a single list in the phone drawer. A test now fails if the header would
  show anything but the three, or if a section is split.
- **The icons left the header.** GitHub is an entry under Open source and stays in the licence line of every footer.
  LinkedIn, the newsroom, About, Contact and Privacy are in a new site line in every footer.
- **The home footer follows the review:** Product (with the roadmap under it), Open source, Trust (the claim ledger,
  what we don't claim, release history, privacy) and Company (about, contact, the newsroom, LinkedIn). Admin moves
  to the bottom line.
- **The desk leaves the public menu:** the demo walkthrough, the LinkedIn desk, engagements and the two home drafts.
  So does Admin. They are linked from the admin console's home under *The desk*, and Admin from every footer.

Where this differs from the review: *approvals* is not listed under Product, because the site sells no approval
feature. The version chip stays in the header, because it is how a reader dates the page in front of them. The newsroom
is in the footer and also under How it works, since nothing was to be lost. What was not done: no page was merged,
renamed or removed, and no page's content changed.
