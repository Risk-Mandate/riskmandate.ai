# v1.34.19 — The stories under one folder, and the vault they will be made in

26 September 2026. A patch: the stories section moved and grew a section; nothing the site sells changed.

## The stories live at /stories/

Everything a story is made of is now in one folder, `site/stories/`, served as
[riskmandate.ai/stories/](https://riskmandate.ai/stories/): the cast and story data, the images, the
pages and their markdown twins, and the board. The old addresses (`stories.html`, `story-<slug>.html`)
redirect. In the header, Articles and Stories share a *Reading* entry, the seventh top-level entry;
the cap holds. The articles page points at the stories in a line.

Under it, three things became folder-aware: the shared menu (a page in a folder sets `RM.data.base`
and the menu links from the site root; that is one module changed on every page), the generator (a
folder's index is addressed as the folder in the sitemap, the twins and the 404 list), and the page
tests (they read every folder `pages.json` names, and resolve a link the way a browser does).

## Who does what, and the board

Section 06 of the stories page says how a story is made from now on: three parties who never share a
session, the lead, a studio model and this site's agent as publisher, working through one encrypted
sgit vault in sgraph.ai's Email-FS-lite protocol. A request is a message, a deliverable is a file, a
task is a file in one of three folders, and one round of work is one commit. The board is derived
from those files, never dragged, and the same board is drawn inside the vault.

The vault is not born yet, and the page says so. Its first content waits in the repository under
`stories-vault/seed/`: the folders, one brief per party, five messages from the publisher (four to
the studio, one to the lead), five tasks, and the page that draws the board. The tooling is
`scripts/stories/mail.mjs`, the protocol in one file with no dependencies. The design is
`docs/briefs/architecture__the-stories-vault-and-the-three-way-workflow.md`, on the console.

## What is not done

The vault's birth, which needs the key; the check-in on a schedule, which needs the key and a token in
the environment and the lead's word on what a scheduled run may decide alone; the studio's first
reply; stories.sgit.ai.
