## The register, played live

The lead asked whether the risk propagation visualiser could be drawn for the business cases, as a
visual example of the risks a product reduces and the ones it introduces ([D27](briefs.html)). It
can, because the cases already compute the same objects.

- **Every case page now carries the figure**, as its section 05. The model's ten roles up to the
  board; every risk that holds for the stated deployment, placed with the roles the model assigns
  it; the corporate register above them; and, at the bottom, each answer the product changes, as a
  switch, with what it adds as switches too.
- **The page carries the model and the engine.** The three rules the build runs (answers to facts,
  facts to risks, risks to corporate) are in the page, ported unchanged, so a switch recomputes the
  register in the browser exactly as the build computed it for the tables. Checked on three cases:
  the counts match the tables with every switch on and with none.
- **Red holds, faded retired, amber brought.** A role's circles count what it holds now and what
  it no longer holds. A change's edges run to the risks it retires, at the margin, given the other
  switches. Click a role, a risk, a change or the product and the walk goes one way per hop, as on
  the article's figure; the panel says why a risk is on the register, what establishes it in the
  model's own facts, what ends it, and which change touched it.
- Nothing is scored. What was not done: the acceptance loop, because the model has no acceptance
  records; the figure shows the register, not who signed for it.
