# v1.38.8 — Review feedback: a device instead of an email, and the first replies

9 October 2026. The lead reviewed the page on an iPad as well as the iPhone, and sent two notes with it: the
email field is not needed, and the two stage pictures did not load.

- **A device or a reference, instead of an email.** The page asks for a name and a device or reference. It
  guesses the device from the browser (iPadOS, which calls itself a Mac, is told apart by its touch screen),
  offers iPhone, iPad, Android, Laptop and Desktop, and still takes free text. An email typed before is
  dropped from the browser's copy. The reader's line in the message, the debug column, the drain's summary
  and the vault's dashboard name the reader with their device.
- **A picture that fails to load is tried twice more,** and every failure and recovery is an event, so it can
  be seen in the debug column and on the dashboard. The stage pictures are well formed and served as WebP like
  the others; why they failed on the iPad is not known, and this is how it will be found if it happens again.
- **A send made as the tab goes away is kept alive** by the browser when it is small enough, so it is not cut
  off; the iPad's messages showed some events arriving twice, which the dashboard already removes.
- **The first replies.** One note to each of the lead's two browsers, the iPhone and the iPad, each encrypted
  to that browser's own reply key and published in `assets/review/replies/`. Each shows at the top of the
  review page in its own browser and nowhere else.

Tested in Chromium with iPhone and iPad profiles, with the stage picture made to fail once: it recovered on
the retry and both events were recorded. Not tested on the devices themselves.
