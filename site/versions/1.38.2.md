# v1.38.2 — A visual diff of the home page, and the questions that decide it

9 October 2026. The lead asked for the before and after of the proposed home page made visual, so that a
reviewer does not have to play spot the difference: screenshots side by side with arrows and highlights, a
slider over the same section, a short sequence from now to proposed, A/B testing, and feedback that comes
back to us with events and comments. And a pricing hypothesis to test with it.

- **`/home-diff.html`, private and unindexed.** Six changed sections, each shown three ways, with one switch
  for all of them. Side by side: what left is marked in red, what arrived in green, numbered, with arrows
  from each old line to its new one. Slider: one screenshot over the other, a handle that wipes from now to
  proposed, the marks on both layers. Four steps: now, what will change with the marks pulsing, what
  changed, proposed, crossfading and playing while the section is on screen. Every section lists its
  changes as old line and new line, and asks which reads better. Then the two new sections, keep or remove;
  the prices as A, B, C or D; every section of the proposed page, keep, remove or change; and which of the
  three ways of showing a change worked best.
- **The pictures and the marks are generated, not drawn.** `scripts/review/capture-home-diff.mjs` takes the
  same section from both pages at the same width, pads the pair to one size, and records every piece of
  text that is on one page and not the other. Each change is paired with the one that shares most of its
  words, so an arrow goes from an old sentence to the sentence that replaced it.
- **Feedback comes back encrypted.** The page keeps everything in the browser as it happens and sends it,
  encrypted in the browser to the site agent's key, into the comms vault's site lane, the same lane and
  envelope the contact form uses: when the reader presses *Send feedback*, and every two minutes while
  there is something new. What it records is listed on the page. The drain accepts the new message type.
  Nothing goes to an analytics service.
- **The lead's pricing hypothesis, as four variants.** The £10 and £50 levels are not selling: lead with the
  levels people buy, and add two engagements that are time with a forward-deployed engineer, £5,000 for a
  first pass and £25,000 for an MVP, delivered by Dinis Cruz. A is now; B is the hypothesis; C is leaner;
  D shows all six.
- **`/engagements.html`, private: the two engagements as a proposed page.** What each delivers, taken only
  from what the interview says exists today; what they do not commit to; and who delivers, named.

Not done: the live home page and the pricing page are unchanged, and the engagements are not on sale. The
feedback is read by the session that holds the comms vault's key, not by this one.
