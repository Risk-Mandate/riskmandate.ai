## The hero says buy, and the study is a review in the console

Two things, both asked for by the lead.

**The hero's two buttons say what they do.** *Find your behaviour policy* is now **Buy an Agent
Behaviour Policy**, going to the store, and *Read a free example* is now **See Policy examples**,
going to [the page that publishes all sixteen](agent-behaviour-policy.html). The same two actions
appear three times on the page — the anchor strip, the hero and the closing panel — and all three
now carry the same words. The longer label is ten pixels wider than the hero column, so the pair's
padding and gap are tightened rather than letting the row stack; between 1081 and 1100px it still
wraps, which is what the design does at that width anyway.

**The synthetic-user study is now a review in the admin console**, not only a page on the site.
`docs/briefs/review__five-synthetic-users-read-the-new-home-page.md` is rendered at
[admin → briefs](admin/briefs/) under *Reviews*, beside the other things read against a named
source. It carries the measurements, the two findings that matter, the four fixed the same day, and
a table of what is still open with whose call each one is. The vault it reads against opens
read-only with the key printed on [the study page](synthetic-users.html).

One note on the second label, recorded rather than argued: *Policy* alone is the word the
[study](synthetic-users.html) measured two readers taking for an insurance policy, which is why the
rest of the page says *behaviour policy*. It stands here because the button beside it says *Agent
Behaviour Policy* in full, and because it is the lead's wording.
