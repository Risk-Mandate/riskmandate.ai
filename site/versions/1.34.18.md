## Stories

The lead's memo of 26 September ([D29](briefs.html)), with three pieces drawn by a ChatGPT image
model: merchandise can wait; the thing to build is stories. A cast, a workflow like the film
industry's, storyboards that carry the narrative and the punchline, and only then the image
models, of which there are several. Later it moves to stories.sgit.ai; for now it is here.

- **[The stories section](stories.html)**: why a story is remembered where an argument is filed;
  the seven-step workflow, with the picture last; the cast of nine as drawn, six people and three
  things, each with the line it was drawn with and what it stands for on this site's model, so a
  story can be written against the model rather than against a picture; one character proposed,
  the one who signs; the rules for a story; and what is not done.
- **Six stories, each a data file with its page.** Two drawn, *Just a draft* and *Just a little
  research*, with the picture, the storyboard it should have followed, and what to correct: the
  broken padlock in the second is a break-in, and the story is about the keys the agent already
  holds. Four storyboards from the articles, not yet drawn: *Tidy my calendar* (delete is
  recoverable, edit is forever), *The pilot worked* (then somebody asked what else it could do),
  *Nobody decided* (silence is not a decision, it is an escalation) and *Always allow* (an approval
  prompt is not a human in the loop). Each page carries the truth under the story, the panels with
  scene, action, dialogue and caption, the prompt for any image model with a copy button, and the
  one panel that would sell on its own.
- **A generator with a check in CI.** A story is a JSON file; the build refuses a line for a
  character who is not in the story's cast, an unknown cast member, or the word *rung*.

What was not done: any model but ChatGPT's; the four storyboards drawn; the one who signs; voice.
