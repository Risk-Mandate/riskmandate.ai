# v1.35.10 — The early-adopters thank you, and improvements for later

2 October 2026. A patch: one page, one programme document, and a reframing on the stories.

## The early adopters

[Early adopters: a thank you](https://riskmandate.ai/early-adopters.html), in *Who it's for* beside the early-access
page. Two groups put something in before there was much to see: the early users of The Cyber Boardroom, who bought
credits and, by the logs, used little of them, and the early adopters of RiskMandate, who said yes to an invitation.
The Cyber Boardroom is the company behind RiskMandate, and the page says so with its mark beside ours. Each group gets
the same gift: an Agent Behaviour Policy for one agent they run, corrected for their situation with them, the level the
store sells for £500, at no cost, for thirty days from the day their email arrives. The route is the existing
early-access form with a code (`CB-` for the first group, `EA-` for the second); the page says what level 3 delivers,
what we ask in return, the five steps, what it is and what it is not.

The operational half is on the console: the two emails, the reply, the reminder after twenty days, the columns the
two lists need, who does what across the six roles, and what needs the lead: the lists, the send date, the order of
the two groups, and the company's legal name if the page should carry it. No email is sent by this agent.

## Improvements for later

Every drawn story carried its notes under *What to correct*. They are now *Improvements for later*, with a line
saying what they are: changes for the next time the story is drawn, none of which stops the picture being used as it
is. The stories index collects them in one section, so the next brief to an image model can start from them.
