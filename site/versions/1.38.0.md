# v1.38.0 — OWASP: the Agent Behaviour Policies proposed as an OWASP project, kept in public

8 October 2026. The founders decided today to propose the Agent Behaviour Policy to OWASP as an open
project, with RiskMandate as its sponsor. The lead, in a voice note: *in a way it's a simple move
because everything is already published … but I think this is going to help a lot the project.* And:
*this should go on the public website, the public Git repo.* A brief written with Perplexity the same
day set the discipline: prepare, do not migrate; nothing external without approval. This release is
the preparation, in public.

- **A new top-level section, [OWASP](https://riskmandate.ai/owasp/).** Eight pages: the
  [overview](https://riskmandate.ai/owasp/), the [charter](https://riskmandate.ai/owasp/charter.html)
  (mission, five objectives, who it is for, what it ships, and eight things it will never claim, no
  score first), [RiskMandate and the project](https://riskmandate.ai/owasp/riskmandate.html) (what moves
  and what stays, who manages what, a commercial model written so a competitor can follow it, what
  OWASP's own policy already settles, and the conflict of interest said first),
  [what moves](https://riskmandate.ai/owasp/contributions.html),
  [the other OWASP projects](https://riskmandate.ai/owasp/ecosystem.html),
  [what other projects teach](https://riskmandate.ai/owasp/lessons.html),
  [the application](https://riskmandate.ai/owasp/application.html) and
  [the tracker](https://riskmandate.ai/owasp/tracker.html).
- **One record behind every status.** `owasp/project.json` holds the five gates, 25 steps, the
  submissions, the people to talk to, where everything lives, the decisions, the open questions and a
  dated log. The pages are rendered from it, so the tracker and the overview cannot disagree, and the
  file is written to move into the project's own repository unchanged. The builder refuses a claim
  that the project is approved, endorsed or official until the record says OWASP accepted it.
- **What moves, asset by asset.** [contribution-inventory.csv](https://riskmandate.ai/owasp/contribution-inventory.csv):
  82 assets from this repository and the sgit sites, 31 to contribute, 13 to rewrite, 11 deferred
  (they need somebody's consent, or are not the ABP), 27 kept with RiskMandate. Every one is
  *unresolved* for rights until somebody checks it; a footer is not proof.
- **What the reading changed.** OWASP's policy makes leadership personal and forbids a project holding
  money or signing with a company, and the committee's good practice asks that leaders not all work for
  one company: so a third leader from outside RiskMandate is a step. The material to move is the model
  at abp.sgit.ai, v0.12.1, not the vault copies here, pinned at 0.3.0. No JSON Schema file exists, and
  no CC BY licence file exists in this repository. The Agent Control Standard joined the GenAI Security
  Project on 1 September; the ecosystem page sets out how an ABP and ACS would meet, and says no
  integration exists.
- **The menu.** OWASP takes the seventh top-level slot; *Try it* moves into *Behaviour policies*.
- **Two documents registered**, D30 (the voice note) and D31 (the brief), byte for byte, with their digests.

The second number moves because the site gained a section and, in a sense, gave something away.

Not done, deliberately: nothing has been sent to OWASP. The email to the Project Committee and the
Agentic Security Initiative, and every field of the request, are drafted on the application page and
wait on the leads. No repository has been created. The name is open: the voice note says *application*
behaviour policies, the brief says *Agent*, and OWASP's good practice against a name confused with a
company's service bears on a name RiskMandate sells under today. Leadership membership is as the lead
stated it, not checked. The ACS observations in the brief are not repeated, because they have not been
re-read at a commit.
