# v1.40.2 — Four animations, from the gap to the kernel, self-hosted

3 November 2026. The lead asked whether the ABP and SHarness animations on ruv.sgit.ai and
the-ultimate-insider.presentation.sgit.ai had been reviewed. They had not been, properly. They have now. Four of them
tell the story this site now tells, in order: the ABP, the ABP enforced, the PBP held by the kernel, and what stays
open until the launcher exists. Those four are on the site.

- **Copied, not linked.** The site loads nothing from another origin, so the four slides live under
  `assets/anim/`, with their slide engine, colour tokens and data, at the source's own relative paths. Every file was
  read before it was copied: the slides fetch only their own data files, and nothing in the data is a key.
- **The four:** E09, mandate against grant; the ABP gate, a port of ruv.sgit.ai's studio v4 (eleven Ruflo actions,
  all allowed by default, four denied and two held for approval under the ABP); E11, the kernel was told first
  (`sharness-ai hello`, six of six held, 25 October 2026); E12, the radius contained, which its own title marks as
  designed, not today.
- **In this site's words.** E09 said *delta*; it now says *gap*. E11 and E12 said *the policy*; they now say *the
  PBP*. E11's footnote now names RiskMandate as SHarness's maintainer. Each changed file says what changed in a
  comment at its top. `assets/anim/README.md` lists every file, its source, its licence (CC BY 4.0 for the talk's
  pieces, Apache-2.0 for the engine and the port) and whether it changed.
- **Where they show:** a new section 03 on `secure-harness.html`, *Watch it*, a single player with four chapters;
  E11 in the home page's SHarness section; E11 as slide 8 of the positioning deck, which plays when shown and leaves
  the arrow keys with the deck; and the demo walkthrough, as the fallback if the browser kernel is slow to boot.
- **`assets/anim/embed.js`** starts a slide when half of it is on screen, pauses it when it scrolls away, and replays
  it on request. Under reduced motion the engine shows the last frame.

What was not done: none of the runs was repeated here. The numbers are the sources' own, dated as they dated them.
The other pieces reviewed stay where they are. These are ruv.sgit.ai's studio versions, the equation, the journey,
E01, E02, E13, and the M01 and M02 story animations. Any of them can come in the same way.
