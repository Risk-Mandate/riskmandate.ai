# v1.41.0 — We can control your agents: the home page on the Web Summit record, one price ladder, the archive

17 November 2026. On 16 November the lead dictated one brief for riskmandate.ai and agenticcells.ai together. In his
words, the old site "was trying to sell stuff that wasn't actually usable … there wasn't real world examples", and
"we have been running agent behavior policies on production, and we have a whole bunch of data". The message is now
simple: we can control your agents, from one to hundreds. He said yes to the merge, the home page, the new ladder and
the archive the same night, and "go" in the conductor's session on 17 November.

- **The home page is the Web Summit record.** "We can control your agents." Four counts from the summit, each linked
  to its source: 191 ABP documents across three of the team's sites (overlaps not removed), 2,262 run reports, 2,980
  actions logged as outside an agent's grant (logged and allowed, not blocked), and one rule that failed and became a
  barrier. Then four situations from our own agents, the film, three photos and motion clips from the stand and the
  arena, the honest order of strength (a policy, then hooks, then a boundary with [SHarness](secure-harness.html)),
  the four ways to work with us, the book, and what was not done. [The home page](index.html).
- **Pricing is one ladder, the same as agenticcells.ai.** £500 for a behaviour policy run by our own agents, low touch;
  £1,500 worked through with you, two meetings and a professional's signature; £5,000 for about two days, your agents
  mapped and controlled; £20,000 for a customer engagement. The first two keep their payment links; the other two start
  with a conversation. **The £10 pack and the £50 vault are retired**: offered from 15 September, they did not sell, and
  the lead read that as the signal. [Pricing](pricing.html).
- **Web Summit 2026 has its own section**, unlisted: [the record](web-summit-2026/), every figure sourced and dated,
  figures that disagree printed side by side; [eighteen situations](web-summit-2026/situations.html), what our agents
  did and what we did about it; [the site plan](web-summit-2026/site-plan.html) and [the newsroom map](web-summit-2026/newsroom-map.html),
  243 newsroom.sgit.ai items with a place on this site.
- **The archive starts.** [The archive](archive/) keeps pages that no longer say what we sell, at their addresses with a
  dated note: the previous home page (v1.36.0 to v1.40.3) is at `archive/home-2026-11-16.html`; the pages for the two
  retired levels and the September Startup Summit pages are listed there.
- **The book.** *One Human, Nine Agents, Nineteen Hours*, the day nine agents and the lead built a talk: the full draft
  (release candidate 2) is hosted beside its source, and the Leanpub page linked.
- **A team on an hourly loop.** Six roles (developer, designer, sherpa, synthetic users, ambassador, researcher) as
  sub-agents of one conductor session; the loop and its backlog are task brief T18.
- **Under the hood.** The home page test now checks that each hero count links to the record and that the template
  count matches the catalogue, in place of the previous hero's vault counts; the pitch deck takes its gap figure from
  the archived home page; the menu entry reads *Four ways in, and pricing*.

What was not done. The summit counts have not been checked by anybody outside the team, and the home page says so;
most summit ABPs were in observe mode and documented rather than measured. No customer is named, because none has
agreed to be. Captions name nobody individually. The Lab page that mentions a corrected policy "from £10" is a dated
edition and is unchanged. Only the old home page has moved into the archive folder so far; other pages are listed there
and keep their addresses. The pricing page still scrolls sideways on a phone, as it did before this release. The menu
still says *How it works*: the site plan's *In action* rename waits.
