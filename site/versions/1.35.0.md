# v1.35.0 — The team: a behaviour policy for each of our own agents

26 September 2026. The second number moves: a new section, about the people and agents that make the site.

## The team, at /team/

One person and two agents make riskmandate.ai, and from today the agents are described the way the site
describes any agent. [The team](https://riskmandate.ai/team/) is in the header under *Behaviour policies*:
who does what; three tiers of information, public, private and secret, each with its rule and what stands in
the way; every surface with its tier; the seven-step workflow that moves a story from the private vault to the
public site, with the evidence each step leaves; the controls, checks and tooling in place, and the three that
are not.

Two Agent Behaviour Policies, in the same grammar as the sixteen in the library: 23 primitives, four barriers
named by the enforcer test, six evidence tiers, three undo classes, the delta derived and never authored.
[The RiskMandate Publisher](https://riskmandate.ai/team/publisher.html), the agent in this repository and the
only party that reads the vault and writes the site: seventeen rows observed on the thing itself, four in
excess, three unbounded. [The RiskMandate Designer](https://riskmandate.ai/team/designer.html), the model
that writes and draws the stories: seven rows, every one observed from what it did on 26 September, two in
excess, both unbounded, because the account's token and the vault key sit in a conversation. Each page ends
with the prompt a clean session starts from, with a copy button, and with the business case for a control
that would move the unbounded count, with no verdict in it.

## What holds it

A new test: nothing private reaches the public tree. No message file and no message id from the vault
anywhere under `site/`, checked before every merge and in CI before every deploy. The builder refuses a grant
row without a barrier, an evidence tier, an undo class and a note, a mandate that wants and refuses the same
row, and anything shaped like a credential. `.claude/agents/publisher.md` is the publisher's role in the form
a Claude Code session loads by name; `/publisher-check-in` is the prompt a routine would fire.

## In the vault

The designer's identity is `designer.chatgpt` from today, renamed before anybody had read a message. It has
been asked, by message, to argue with its policy row by row and to draw the team page as an infographic in
the cast's style.

## What is not done

The policies as vaults in the catalogue; a write token scoped to one vault, asked of the sgit team; the
scheduled check-in, until the key and the token are environment secrets and the lead has said what a
scheduled run may decide alone; the designer's first reply.
