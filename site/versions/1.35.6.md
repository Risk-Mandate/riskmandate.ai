## The consent line says what it means

**The lead sent the first message through the contact form, and it arrived**: encrypted in his
browser, written to the lane, drained, decrypted and filed in the comms vault at 10:51 UTC on
30 September. He also read the box under the form and asked what it was trying to say. It was written
from our side of the table, as an instruction to us, which is not how a person reads a box they are
about to tick. Both forms now say it from the reader's side: *I am happy for RiskMandate to keep this
message in its private vault and to reply to me by email; I can ask for it to be deleted at any time;
nobody outside RiskMandate reads it, and my address is not added to any list.* The early-access box
says the same and adds the vault we build from it and that it is not published without their yes.
The warning shown when the box is not ticked says why it is there. Nothing else changes.
