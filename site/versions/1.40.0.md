# v1.40.0 — Enforcement, when you want it: SHarness, the film, and the demo

3 November 2026. Since 29 October there is SHarness, an open-source secure harness that RiskMandate maintains
and a company runs in its own environment, which holds the rules in an Agent Behaviour Policy with the
Linux kernel. The lead: it *"changes quite a bit the offering and the positioning"*. This release says so,
with its status beside it, puts Silvio Switha's film on the home page, and prepares the demo of 4 November
and the Web Summit stand of 12 November.

- **The positioning.** RiskMandate writes the ABP; the customer's environment enforces it, with the
  controls it already runs or with SHarness. RiskMandate is still never in the request path: the policy
  compiles once, at launch, and costs nothing per request. The home page hero and the gate's caption say
  so.
- **A new home page section, *Enforcement, when you want it*:** every rule is told (a line in the
  prompt), checked (a hook) or held (a boundary); a diagram of SHarness, from the ABP to a Process
  Behaviour Policy named by a hash you supply, to the launcher, the kernel and five doors; and what runs
  today beside what is still the design, as at 29 October, 0.2.0.
- **A new page, `secure-harness.html`:** what changes, the three things that make a policy binding, the
  offer in four steps (describe, check, hold, show), the status in full, and the questions a sceptical
  engineer asks. The status, in the project's own words: *"Nothing yet binds the agent itself."*
- **The film.** *Reach, Mandate, Gap*, 53 seconds, by Silvio Switha, under the home page hero: self-hosted
  as MP4 and WebM with a poster, so nothing loads from another site. It says *delta* where this site says
  *gap*.
- **Web Summit.** A strip on the home page: Lisbon, 9 to 12 November, the stand on Thursday 12 November,
  meetings and the films on web-summit.sgit.ai.
- **The demo:** a private walkthrough (`walkthrough.html`), twenty minutes in nine steps with what to say,
  the questions to expect and what to rehearse; and a fourteen-slide deck at
  `assets/deck/positioning/`, with speaker notes behind N, full screen on F and print on P.
- **From the newsroom** gains a group on the secure harness, four articles. **The LinkedIn desk** gains the
  film, SHarness and Web Summit, fifteen items in all.
- **A brief for the session that maintains customers.riskmandate.ai**, mapping the same story there: a
  seventh step that takes a line from told to held, the ladder on *What is an ABP?*, the film, the seven
  open fixes of 23 October, a demo path for the stand, and the decision on writing the first ABP as a
  service, which is the lead's.

What was not done: SHarness was not run from this site, and nothing here claims it binds the agent; the
other pages that say RiskMandate does not enforce still say so, which stays true of RiskMandate itself,
and will be reworded with SHarness beside them in a later release. The film has no licence stated on the
Web Summit site; it is here because the lead sent it for this page.
