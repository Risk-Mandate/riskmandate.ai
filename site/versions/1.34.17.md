## Merch for people who hold the risk

The lead has a Merch on Demand account and wants RiskMandate shirts and hoodies that a stranger
would buy: funny lines about risk, some with a small figure, the mark present, a gift for
somebody who holds the risk at work ([D28](briefs.html)).

- **[A brief for an image model](admin/briefs/merch__funny-designs-for-people-who-hold-the-risk/index.html)**,
  on the console with a markdown twin to hand over by link. What RiskMandate is in one paragraph
  the model may use; who buys the shirt and why; the brand kit and where the files are; the print
  rules, with Amazon's artwork specification stated as known and to be confirmed on the account;
  what a design must never carry, which are the site's own rules on cloth; forty-two taglines in
  six families, each with the joke explained and who it lands with; twelve concepts, six with a
  figure and six type only; the prompt to paste, one design at a time; and what to send back.
- The lines are plays on things the site actually says, so a buyer who looks us up finds the line
  is also true: *There is no deny button*, *Accepted ≠ Acceptable*, *Hope is not a control*,
  *It can do 340 things. You authorised 12.*, *Risk holder (temporary)*, *Delete is recoverable.
  Edit is forever.*

What was not done: the designs, which the model makes and the lead chooses; and the trade-mark
search on each line before it goes on sale, which the brief asks for.
