# v1.39.0 — Try it first: the customers site, the newsroom, and LinkedIn

21 to 23 October 2026. RiskMandate has a LinkedIn page from 21 October, and the traffic it brings should go
where people can try an Agent Behaviour Policy: customers.riskmandate.ai, the site for the first customers,
which this site had not linked until now. The lead also asked for the newsroom's latest thinking to be
brought here, and for the articles to publish on LinkedIn, in order, ready to paste.

- **Every page's header button is now "Try it free"**, a link to customers.riskmandate.ai, where six examples
  put a behaviour policy on Claude or ChatGPT in five minutes. It was "Book a demo" or "Get in touch"; those
  buttons inside pages still open the contact form.
- **The home page takes the wording the review chose.** The hero no longer says the policies are enforced
  in real time: two newsroom articles pointed out that it put RiskMandate in the request path, which it is
  not. The lead chose the proposed wording on every section they answered; the stage, the builder, bring your
  policy, the prompt and the alerts now say that your own controls enforce the rules. The two new sections of
  the proposal (who does what, the ten questions) are left out, as the other reader voted. Three calls to
  try it: the hero, a new band after the stage (ask, add the line, new chat), and the close.
- **From the newsroom (`newsroom.html`, under Reading):** fifteen articles from the SGit Newsroom that say most
  simply what an ABP is, where its line sits and where it is going, each retold in a paragraph with a short
  quote, a date and a link. With the news that RiskMandate is on LinkedIn.
- **A LinkedIn link** beside the GitHub one in every page's header, and in the home page's footer.
- **The LinkedIn desk (`linkedin.html`, private):** twelve items in the order to publish them, the profile
  entries for both founders, the page's first post, the founders' posts, five articles and two link posts,
  each combining newsroom pieces with screenshots, each ending at the customers site. Each copies as LinkedIn
  takes it: an article as rich text with its pictures, a post as plain text, a profile field by field, and the
  HTML. Nimay's entries are drafts for him to change.
- **The workflow, written down and partly automated:** items in `scripts/linkedin/items.mjs`, pictures taken by
  `scripts/linkedin/capture.mjs` (the customers site and the newsroom fetched through curl, which checks their
  certificates), the desk built by `scripts/linkedin/build.mjs`, whose check runs with every build and whose
  to-do list names the site's articles with no LinkedIn item yet: eight today.
- **Try it** now starts with the customers site; the rules remain the next step.

What was not done: nothing is posted to LinkedIn from here, and LinkedIn offers no way to; the lead pastes.
Whether pasted pictures carry into LinkedIn's article editor was not tested; each picture also has its own copy
button. The customers site is linked and quoted, not changed.
