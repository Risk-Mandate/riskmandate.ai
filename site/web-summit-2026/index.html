Web Summit 2026 · the record

RiskMandate in action, on our own agents.

From 9 to 12 November 2026 in Lisbon, a team of people and their agents ran a stand, a newsroom and an incident site. Every agent on that team had an Agent Behaviour Policy (ABP) written in this site’s grammar: what it can reach, what it was authorised to do, the gap, and what stands in the way. This page is the mapping of what that left on the public record, with every figure sourced and dated.

Nothing here is scored. A count is a count, and where two of our own pages give different counts, both are printed and neither is chosen.

As at 16 November 2026, every source read that day between 10:00 and 11:30 UTC. Our sources only: the team’s public sites and the public repositories behind them. No private record, no contact detail, nothing measured on anybody else’s system.

One for every agent, in three places.

What was not done. None of these ABPs blocks anything by design: every one is in an observe or explorer mode, and that was the lead’s instruction (“Blocking should always be the last worst case scenario,” as the board quotes it). The Storm Charles set is marked as drafts the lead has not agreed. The Fringe repository’s ABP folder was not counted. Most of the 94 are documented rather than measured: the grant is what each agent’s brief and tools say, not what was tested on it.

What this shows, and what it does not. It shows the loop the ABP exists for, run on ourselves: a mandate written per agent, the gap logged on every run, and one gap turned into a barrier the agent cannot remove. It does not show prevention at scale: almost every out-of-grant action was logged and allowed, by design. Nobody outside the team has checked these numbers.

09 · The homepage, proposed

RiskMandate in action at Web Summit 2026.

A proposal. The homepage has not changed and will not until the lead approves. A private draft is at home-proposal. The shape: the summit leads, because it is the first time the ABP ran on a team of this size rather than on a template.

  1. The opening. “Give agents access. Not free rein.” kept as the line; under it, the team at work as one picture (the circuit or the team map, as a still, dated) and the film.
  2. The record, in four counts. ABPs published, run reports, gaps logged, the one rule turned barrier: each linked to its source here, each with its date, none scored.
  3. The loop, once. The push to main on 2 November, the barrier on 6 November: the clearest single story of reach, mandate and gap.
  4. What you can do now. The ABP maker and the sixteen free examples, then the levels as priced today.
  5. What was not done. Observe mode, not blocking; documented, not measured; nobody outside the team has checked the counts. Said on the homepage, not only here.
10 · How this page was made

Read, counted, not tested.

Four read-only passes on 16 November: this site’s own pages; the team site (all 756 sitemap pages as markdown); PrompTugal.news, Storm Charles and the PrompTugal board through a public clone; the stand site and newsroom.sgit.ai. GET requests and a public clone only; nothing was submitted, probed or measured on any system. No private vault, sealed list or customer record was read.
