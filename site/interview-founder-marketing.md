Sixteen example policies are published free, for tools such as Claude Code, ChatGPT and Gmail connectors. The context: insurers are starting to exclude AI from cover, and businesses answer for what their agents do. RiskMandate is UK-based, early-stage and founder-led, and it is not an insurer.

2. Whether the name "RiskMandate" works.
3. Who to reach first, and where they gather.
4. Which UK events matter, and how to get on stage or into the room.
5. Content angles and formats that would spread.
6. A practical action plan for the next thirty, sixty and ninety days.
