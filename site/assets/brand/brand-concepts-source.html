Guidelines 05 — VoiceVoice.dc.html
Brand guidelines · 05
Voice
You are selling to the person who will be asked to sign. Write like an underwriter, not a startup.
Mechanism, not claim
Every assertion names how it works. "A token bypasses the password and the second factor" — never "80% of breaches".
Terse
Short sentences. One idea each. If a sentence needs a comma to survive, it needs a full stop instead.
Defensible
No certification we do not hold, no percentage without a source, no competitor named. A GRC buyer checks.
Say this
Make your agents insurable.
A real risk can't be denied, only accepted — for an interval, by an owner.
The grant is not the mandate.
We begin where security stops.
Not this
Revolutionise your AI security posture.
Eliminate 80% of agent risk.
Block rogue agents instantly.
The leading AI governance platform.
The one-sentence test
Could the Head of Risk repeat this sentence to their board without having to defend it? If not, cut it.

insurable · owner · expiry · record
