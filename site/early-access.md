# A behaviour policy for the agent you actually run, made for you, and yours to correct.

A small programme for people who already run an agent with real access: a mailbox, a code host, a workflow tool, a CRM. Each member gets **an Agent Behaviour Policy for one agent, as an encrypted vault they hold the keys to**, and is asked to do one thing with it: say where it is wrong. It is free, it is by invitation, and the invitation carries a code. If you have one, register below.

## One agent, written down, in a vault of your own.

The sixteen published examples describe deployments in general. Yours describes your organisation and the agent you named: what it can reach, documented from the vendor's own pages and dated; what you would authorise it to do, drafted for you; the gap between the two, derived; and what stands in the way of each thing. Then you correct the mandate, because only you can, and the gap recomputes.

An encrypted vault with the reading app inside. The key arrives by a separate message, never on a page. A read key you may hand to your board, your auditor or your broker.

Every vault carries `MAP-A-GRANT.md`: paste it into the agent that holds the credential and it measures what the credential lets it do, in the same vocabulary. Twenty minutes.

Corrections, questions and what broke go to `agent@riskmandate.ai` or through [the contact form](contact.html), and a person answers within a working day.

Volume and instances on the consequence layer, the session mandate, a view per audience. Members see them first and shape them.

- **What we ask.** Run it against the real agent. Tell us where the mandate is wrong, and what you would do with the document. Twenty minutes for the prompts, and a reply; half an hour on a call if you would rather show us.
- **What it is not.** Not a security assessment, and not a promise about your agent's behaviour. A description you can argue with. The arguing is the product, which is why the reviewed levels at the store exist.
- **What it costs.** Nothing. The store sells correction and review; the programme is how we learn what to correct.
- **What we count.** Registrations, then the number of people who ran the prompts and corrected a mandate. Your vault is never listed or published without your yes, and the default is no.
