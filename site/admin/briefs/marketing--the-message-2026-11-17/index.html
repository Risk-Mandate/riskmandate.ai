1. Three drafts of the paragraph

Draft A: proof from our own team (89 words, one figure)

We can control your agents, from one agent to hundreds. For each agent, RiskMandate writes an Agent Behaviour Policy (ABP): what it can reach, what you authorised, the gap, and what stands in the way. Start there: not the strongest control, but a lot of value for money right now. Add hooks and SHarness when you want more. We do it on our own team: more than a hundred agents at Web Summit 2026 (https://web-summit.sgit.ai/team/everything/). Open source, no lock-in. If you need help controlling your agents, we are your person.

Figure: "more than a hundred agents", source the team's own count, https://web-summit.sgit.ai/team/everything/, as linked on the home page (read 17 Nov 2026).

Draft B: plain, no figure (87 words)

If you have AI agents, we can control them, from one agent to hundreds. We start with an Agent Behaviour Policy (ABP) for each one: what it can do, what you authorised, the gap, and what stands in the way. 4. Notes

Recommended: Draft A. It is the lead's order in one breath (control, a policy first, hooks and SHarness, open source, "your person"), and its one figure is our own team, the proof the lead asked to lead with. Use C where the reader should click before talking (a post); B where a link cannot be carried (spoken, a bio).

What each draft deliberately does not claim:

  • All three. No customer, no named person, no score, no conformity word. They do not say the ABP stops an

    attacker (the pricing page says plainly it does not), nor that hooks or SHarness bind every agent (the home page says "nothing yet binds the agent itself" of SHarness). "Control" is the lead's word; none of the drafts says enforce, guarantee or secure.

  • Draft A. It says we do it on our own team, not that every summit agent was blocked or held: the summit ABPs

    were in observe mode, gaps logged and allowed apart from one call, and most were documented rather than measured. It also does not say "each with its own ABP": the stand deck of 10 Nov shows 109 agents, 94 with a written policy (https://web-summit.sgit.ai/meetings/2026-11-10-1600/show/). If A is used where there is room, add "in observe mode" after the link.

  • Draft B. No figure and no proof link, so it claims nothing measurable; it should travel with a link to the home

    page or the situations page wherever one fits.

  • Draft C. "We show our working" points at the free library only; it does not claim the sixteen templates were

    measured (most rows are documented from the vendor's pages, not run).

For the lead (two small things found while drafting):

  1. The lead said "we are your person"; the home page closes with "we are your people". The drafts use the lead's

    word. Which one the site keeps is the lead's call.

  2. The home page's opening says "more than a hundred agents ..., each with its own Agent Behaviour Policy (ABP)",

    while its own stand-deck figure says 94 of 109 had a written policy. A developer task, not a message one; flagged for the conductor.
