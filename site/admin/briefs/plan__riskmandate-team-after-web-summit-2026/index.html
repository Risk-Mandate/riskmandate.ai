1. What the summit gave us

The first time the ABP ran on a team rather than a template. From the mapping, read 16 November: 191 ABP documents across three of the team's sites (94 + 54 + 43, overlaps not removed); 2,262 run reports from 76 agents; 2,980 entries logged as outside an agent's grant; one rule that failed (a push to main, 2 Nov) and became a technical barrier (6 Nov). Every ABP was in observe or explorer mode by the lead's instruction; the Storm Charles set is marked as drafts not agreed. No customer is named anywhere.

That is the asset: a real record of reach, mandate and gap on more than a hundred agents, with its limits written down. The plan is to put it in front of the people the team met, without claiming more than it shows.

4. The homepage proposal

RiskMandate in action at Web Summit 2026. Keep "Give agents access. Not free rein." as the line; under it the team at work as one dated still and the film; four counts, each linked to its source, none scored; the push-to-main story once; then the ABP maker, the sixteen free examples and the levels as priced; and what was not done (observe mode, documented not measured, nobody outside the team has checked) said on the homepage itself. Drafted on a private page; the homepage is not touched until the lead says yes.
