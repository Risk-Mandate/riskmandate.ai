# The risk-acceptance layer for the agent-detection ecosystem.

A large, well-funded field is solving the hard problem of seeing what agents do — their behaviour, their decisions, the control plane. RiskMandate doesn't compete there. We sit on top: the layer that turns those findings into owned, accepted business risk — which is what actually gets fixes funded.

## We don't detect. We don't remediate. We make the business own the risk.

Detection tells you what is happening. RiskMandate makes the business own it — and ownership is what drives the funding and energy to actually fix things.

## Complementary by design, in both directions.

We send partners new business through referral, and we strengthen the case for the products they already sell by deepening their risk analysis. They keep the client relationships and the detection and remediation we don't want. Nobody is competing for the same ground.

## One finding, deepened into a business case.

A partner already detected something. On its own it's a line in a report. Watch RiskMandate turn it into a decision the business will fund.

The partner keeps the client relationship. We just made their finding impossible to ignore.

## When the question is "how," we point out.

RiskMandate isn't in the business of detection or remediation. Our work raises those questions constantly — and the honest answer is a partner. Recommendations are curated for fit, never pay-to-play.

The candidate shortlist and ranking criteria are being defined — kept transparent and honest by design.

## The easiest integration point in the stack.

Because the technology is open source with no lock-in, a partner can adopt as much or as little as suits them, integrate at their own pace, and never fear being trapped. That's a far lower barrier than any closed product.
