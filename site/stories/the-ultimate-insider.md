## What the story says that the site also says.

Three things arrive at once. An agent is a reasoning engine in a loop with tools, and it has skills no insider ever had, so the insider threat that never scaled now does. The systems it sits in were built for code that stays where it was put: no journal, backups by the day, permissions that are the union of everything ever needed. And the discipline meant to decide about both runs in a spreadsheet, reviewed quarterly; because it cannot see what the agent can reach it cannot say what the risk is, so it cannot fund a control, so the answer becomes pull the plug. The way out is the irony at the end: write down what the agent can reach, what it is for, the gap and what stands in the way; put a fence where only a boundary is a control; put a name and a date on the acceptance; and then let it run faster inside that than it ever could outside. The company and the incident are invented; the pattern is the public record of 2025, in the article with its sources.
