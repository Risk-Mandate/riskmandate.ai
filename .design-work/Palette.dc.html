Brand guidelines · 02
Colour
Two grounds carry the brand: ink is where you make the argument, off-white is where you show the record. Green is the only accent that means "accepted".
Grounds
Canvas
#0A0A09
The hero surface. Full-bleed sections, booth panels, the deck.
Ink
#0D0D0C
Cards and the mark's container on dark. Also body type on light.
Document
#F7F6F2
The reading surface — long copy, guidelines, one-pagers.
Document alt
#EFEDE7
Banded sections that need separation without a border.
Accent — use sparingly and only with meaning
Green
#1A7F5A
Primary. Accepted, owned, in-mandate. Buttons and the mark.
Green bright
#22c55e
On ink only, where #1A7F5A is too quiet. Never as a fill on light.
