    The workflow

    Every time we publish here, we publish there.

    1. Write it here first. An article on riskmandate.ai, or a piece on the newsroom. LinkedIn is where people hear of it; the site is where it lives.
    2. Add an item to scripts/linkedin/items.mjs: an article, a post, or a line in a combined piece, with the shots it needs and the customers link at the end.
    3. Take the pictures: node scripts/linkedin/capture.mjs --only <id>, with the site served locally, so a picture shows what will be live.
    4. Build and release: node scripts/linkedin/build.mjs, then a release as for any site change. The pictures must be live before an article is pasted, because it links them.
    5. Publish from this page, on the suggested day. Then set published and url on the item, rebuild and commit, so the record says what went out and where.
    6. Check the gaps: node scripts/linkedin/build.mjs --todo lists the articles still without an item. It runs in every build, and the list above is the same list.

    What this page does not do: it does not post anything. LinkedIn offers no way to publish a company page's articles from a static site, so a person pastes them. The copy buttons are the automation; the record in items.mjs is the memory.
