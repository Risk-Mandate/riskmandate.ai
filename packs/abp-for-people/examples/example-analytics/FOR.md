# An Agent Behaviour Policy for Example Analytics

**Prepared for** A. Reader · **as a starting point, not a finished policy** · 2026-09-24

This vault is an Agent Behaviour Policy for one agent in one deployment: **Claude's Gmail connector**, as
Example Analytics might run it. It writes down four things:

1. **What the agent can reach**, the grant. This part is not about Example Analytics: it is the documented
   capability of this kind of deployment, quoted from the vendor's own pages and dated.
2. **What you would want it to do**, the mandate. This part is our guess, from what Example Analytics
   says about itself in public. It is the part that is most likely wrong, and the part you
   are asked to correct.
3. **The gap between the two**, computed, never written by hand.
4. **What stands in the way** of each capability you did not ask for: nothing, an expectation,
   a setting, or a boundary.

Nothing here is scored, and nothing here was tested on any of Example Analytics's systems.

## Why this deployment

This is a fictional example. For a real organisation this paragraph names the page that showed which assistant they use, or the function that made this deployment the likeliest one, in two or three sentences.

## What we assumed

- That client work runs through a shared Gmail workspace.
- That consultants, not the assistant, send anything to a client.
- That nobody has switched on Calendar or Drive for the assistant.

## What we read

Every source is public and dated; the list is in `data/for.json`.

## What we would like from you

Ten minutes, and whichever of these you have an answer to:

- **Is this the agent you run?** If not, which one is, and we will redo it.
- **Is the mandate right?** Which of the things we said you want, you do not, and the reverse.
- **Which scenario is closest to a real day?** And the one we missed.
- **Would you show this to anybody?** Your security lead, your risk owner, your board. Who, and
  what would they need that is not here?
- **What would make it worth paying for?**

Reply to the person who sent you the link. Everything you tell us stays private unless you say
otherwise, and this vault changes with a date when you correct it.
